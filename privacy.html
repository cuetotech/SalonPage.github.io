Privacy

Privacy Notice

Last updated: August 19, 2026.

This notice explains how dominican.salon uses information when you visit the website. The website is primarily informational and does not offer appointment booking, account registration, or online checkout.

Information we may receive

When you visit the site, basic technical information may be generated automatically, such as browser type, device information, approximate location derived from network information, referring pages, pages viewed, and interactions with site links. If you tap a phone or directions link, your device may open your phone application or a third-party mapping service.

Google advertising measurement

The site uses a Google tag for advertising measurement. Optional advertising and analytics storage is set to denied by default and can be enabled only after you choose “Allow” in the privacy choices shown on the site. Your choice is stored locally in your browser so the site can remember it.

Google may process data according to its own terms and privacy practices. For more information about how Google handles business and advertising data, visit Google's Business Data Responsibility resources and Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

You can decline optional advertising and analytics storage when the privacy prompt appears. You can also reopen the privacy choices from the “Privacy Settings” control in the site footer. Clearing your browser storage may remove your saved preference and cause the prompt to appear again.

Third-party services and links

The website links to external services such as Google Maps and may mention nearby businesses solely to help visitors locate the salon. Those services and businesses have their own terms and privacy practices. Dominican Hair Salon does not control third-party websites or applications.

Data we do not intentionally collect through this site

The website does not intentionally request payment card information, government identification numbers, health information, or online appointment details. Do not send sensitive personal information through website links or third-party services unless you have independently confirmed that the destination is appropriate and secure.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice as the website, advertising tools, or applicable requirements change. The date at the top identifies the latest published version.
